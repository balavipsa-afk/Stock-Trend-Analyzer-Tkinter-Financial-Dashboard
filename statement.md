# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Most people who want to try investing don’t have access to simple or friendly tools that help them make quick, informed decisions. If you’re not a market expert, existing solutions can be overwhelming, loaded with technical features, or don’t let you easily compare top-performing stocks side-by-side. It’s hard to find a tool that gives clear, real-time insights and predictions in a way that makes sense at a glance.

## Scope of the Project

Stock Trend Analyzer is a convenient desktop app designed to fill this gap. Users can select, analyze, and compare several leading stocks—and the S&P 500 [finance:S&P 500] index—without any hassle. The app brings together sharp data visualizations, easy-to-read moving averages, and one-click future price predictions, all packaged inside a modern, intuitive Tkinter interface. Everything runs on your desktop, no web browser required.

## Target Users

- Anyone just starting out with investing who wants to explore stock trends visually
- Students and teachers looking to learn or teach about the stock market and financial data
- Python developers curious about building or experimenting with financial visualizations

## High-Level Features

- Instantly see clear, up-to-date stock charts and moving average trends
- Get a quick estimate for tomorrow’s price using machine learning, no technical background needed
- Discover which stocks are standing out with automatic “best performers” suggestions
- Use and enjoy a stylish, dark-themed interface that’s designed for clarity and comfort—even on long study or trading sessions
